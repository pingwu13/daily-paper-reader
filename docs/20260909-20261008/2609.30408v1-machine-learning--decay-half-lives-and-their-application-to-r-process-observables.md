## 摘要
准确建模高度富中子核的 $β$ 衰变半衰期，是理解 $r$ 过程核合成及由此产生的千新星光变曲线的关键挑战。我们引入一种数据驱动的方法来建模 $β$ 衰变，该方法使用在最新实验测量上训练的混合密度网络（Mixture Density Networks, MDNs）来外推富中子核的半衰期。与标准确定性回归不同，MDN 直接参数化半衰期的概率分布，从而提供内在（偶然）不确定性。我们将单个模型的内在不确定性与通过随机变化输入训练数据样本所产生的一系列模型进行对比。尽管所有模型都与实验数据表现出极好的一致性，但我们发现，改变训练数据集会产生范围很广的外推结果，尤其是在诸如 $N=126$ 同中子素链这类数据匮乏区域。随后，我们将一组选定的模型结果传播到 $r$ 过程网络计算中，以评估它们对同位素丰度的影响。最后，通过将所得同位素丰度与热化效率耦合，我们将有效核加热率转换为全波段光变曲线。我们比较了由单个模型的内在不确定性产生的结果范围，与由在不同训练数据集上训练的多个独立模型产生的结果范围。

## Abstract
Accurately modeling $β$-decay half-lives of highly neutron-rich nuclei is a critical challenge for understanding $r$-process nucleosynthesis and the resulting kilonova light curves. We introduce a data-driven approach to model $β$ decay that uses Mixture Density Networks (MDNs) trained on the latest experimental measurements to extrapolate the half-lives of neutron rich nuclei. Unlike standard deterministic regression, the MDN directly parameterizes the probability distribution of the half-lives, providing intrinsic (aleatoric) uncertainty. We contrast the intrinsic uncertainty of a single model with the range of models produced by using random variations of the input training data samples. While all models show excellent agreement with experimental data, we find that varying the training data sets produces a wide range of extrapolations, particularly along data-poor regions such as the $N=126$ isotonic chain. We then propagate a selection of model results into $r$-process network calculations to evaluate their impact on isotopic abundances. Finally, by coupling the resulting isotopic abundances with thermalization efficiencies, we translate the effective nuclear heating rates into bolometric light curves. We compare the ranges of outcomes produced from the intrinsic uncertainty of a single model to those produced by multiple independent models trained on different training data sets.
